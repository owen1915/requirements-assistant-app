[1.1.1.11]  (Propulsion System Cryptographic Module)
  The propulsion system shall utilize a dedicated hardware module for secure key storage, encryption, and authentication.

[1.1.1.12]  (Fuel System Cryptographic Module)
  The fuel system shall utilize a dedicated hardware module for secure key storage, encryption, and authentication.

[1.1.1.13]  (Electrical System Cryptographic Module)
  The electrical system shall utilize a dedicated hardware module for secure key storage, encryption, and authentication.

[1.1.1.14]  (Communications System Cryptographic Module)
  The communications system shall utilize a dedicated hardware module for secure key storage, encryption, and authentication.

[1.1.1.15]  (Mission Computer Maintenance Ports)
  The mission computer shall have all maintenance ports removed or rendered inoperable when the system is in operation.

[C-1.28]  (Power Management Requirement)
  The MQ-99 Berserker shall be capable of dynamically managing power supply to subsystems, including the ability to safely and promptly cut power to any subsystem upon receiving a designated signal

[C-1.29]  (Final Systems Check)
  The MQ-99 Berserker shall perform a comprehensive check of all systems to confirm operational integrity and readiness before mission launch.

[MR - 12]  (Video Latency)
  The Berserker EO/IR sensor shall transmit video with a latency no greater than 300 milliseconds under nominal operating conditions.

[MR - 13]  (Dropped Frames)
  The Berserker EO/IR Sensor shall not drop more than 5 frames per hour during video transmission.

[MR - 14]  (EO/IR Accuracy)
  The Berserker EO/IR sensor shall achieve a classification accuracy of at least 90% when identifying targets under standard operating conditions.
