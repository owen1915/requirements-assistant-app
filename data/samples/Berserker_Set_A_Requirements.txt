[1]  (Top Level Cyber User Need)
  MQ-99 shall be able to complete assigned missions 90% of the time under cyber attack.

[1.1]  (Hardened (Prevent))
  MQ-99 shall be sufficiently hardened from cyber attack.

[1.1.1]  (Flight Critical Hardware Assurance )
  MQ-99 shall utilize Hardware Assurance techniques including secure boot, cryptographic hardware modules, and removing maintenance ports on flight critical hardware.

[1.1.1.1]  (Mission Computer Secure Boot)
  The mission computer shall boot securely and verify the integrity of the bootloader and firmware before allowing the system to start.

[1.1.1.10]  (Navigation System Cryptographic Module)
  The navigation system shall utilize a dedicated hardware module for secure key storage, encryption, and authentication.

[C-1.26]  (Operational Readiness Requirement)
  The MQ-99 Berserker shall be capable of achieving full operational readiness through staged power-up procedures that ensure all systems are functional and mission-ready within a predefined timeframe.

[C-1.27]  (Electrical Distribution Requirement)
  The MQ-99 Berserker shall continuously provide stable and regulated power to all operational subsystems as required, ensuring uninterrupted system performance throughout mission execution.

[MR - 10]  (Recovery Rate)
  The MQ-99 Berserker UAS shall achieve a recovery rate of at least 85%, ensuring successful return and landing after mission completion under standard operating and environmental conditions.

[MR - 11]  (Survivability)
  The MQ-99 Berserker UAS shall maintain a survivability rate of at least 75% during mission operations in contested environments, accounting for threat detection, avoidance systems, and defensive countermeasures.
